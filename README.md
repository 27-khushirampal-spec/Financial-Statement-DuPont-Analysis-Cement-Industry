# Financial Statement Analysis & DuPont Analysis – Indian Cement Industry

## Project Overview

This project presents a comparative financial statement and **5-Step DuPont Analysis** of four leading Indian cement companies: UltraTech Cement, Shree Cement, ACC Limited, and Ambuja Cements.

The study evaluates financial performance over a five-year period, focusing on profitability, asset utilisation, financial leverage, and Return on Equity (ROE). UltraTech Cement is the primary company, while the other three companies serve as industry peers for comparative analysis.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse the financial performance of UltraTech Cement over five years.
- Compare UltraTech Cement with Shree Cement, ACC Limited, and Ambuja Cements.
- Apply the 5-Step DuPont framework to understand the drivers of ROE.
- Evaluate profitability, asset efficiency, and financial leverage.
- Identify differences in financial performance and operational efficiency across the selected companies.

## Methodology: 5-Step DuPont Analysis

The project uses the 5-Step DuPont framework to decompose Return on Equity into five components.

1. **Tax Burden** = Net Income / Profit Before Tax
2. **Interest Burden** = Profit Before Tax / EBIT
3. **Operating Margin** = EBIT / Revenue
4. **Asset Turnover** = Revenue / Total Assets
5. **Equity Multiplier** = Total Assets / Shareholders' Equity

Together, these components help assess how taxation, financing costs, operating profitability, asset utilisation, and financial leverage influence shareholder returns.

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Intra-Firm Analysis
Five-year analysis of UltraTech Cement's financial performance, focusing on ROE trends, operating margins, asset turnover, and financial leverage.

### 2. Comparative Company Analysis
Application of the same DuPont framework to Shree Cement, ACC Limited, and Ambuja Cements to compare financial performance and identify differences in profitability, efficiency, and leverage.

### 3. Profitability Analysis
Evaluation of operating margins, net profit margins, and ROE to understand the companies' ability to generate returns from their operations.

### 4. Asset Efficiency
Assessment of asset turnover to understand how effectively each company utilises its asset base to generate revenue.

### 5. Financial Leverage
Examination of the equity multiplier and its contribution to shareholder returns, alongside the financial risks associated with leverage.

## Key Findings

- **UltraTech Cement:** Demonstrated scale, financial stability, and improving ROE, supported by operating performance and the use of financial leverage.
- **Shree Cement:** Showed comparatively strong operating profitability, with improvements in margins and ROE primarily associated with operating performance.
- **ACC Limited:** Displayed more variable profitability and returns, highlighting fluctuations in operating performance and asset efficiency.
- **Ambuja Cements:** Demonstrated moderate performance with improvements in profitability and asset utilisation in the later years of the study.

The analysis highlights that ROE should be evaluated through its underlying drivers rather than in isolation. Profitability, efficient asset utilisation, and prudent financial leverage collectively influence sustainable shareholder returns.
